## 1. What Matters Most in a Proposal Ring

A proposal ring needs emotional clarity and practical resilience. The centre stone does not have to be large to look intentional; a balanced setting and polished shoulders often create the strongest impression.

- Solitaire: classic, focused, and easy to wear.
- Halo: more face-up spread within a controlled budget.
- Bezel: modern, protected, and low profile.
- Gold-only statement: meaningful when metal design is the priority.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ₹50,000 enough for a proposal ring?

Yes. It can support attractive gold and diamond-led designs when you prioritise proportion and transparent specifications.

### Is a solitaire or halo better for a proposal?

Solitaire suits quiet, classic taste; halo gives more visual spread. Choose from the recipient's existing jewellery rather than popularity.

### What if I get the size wrong?

Choose a seller with clear resizing or exchange support and preserve all packaging until the fit is confirmed.
