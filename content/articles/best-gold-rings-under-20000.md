## 1. What Your ₹20,000 Budget Can Realistically Buy

At this price, gold content and design complexity compete directly. Expect slim bands, petite solitaires, small textured details, and delicate everyday styles. Intricate halos or heavy solid-gold profiles are less common.

- 14KT often gives more design flexibility per rupee.
- Lightweight 18KT can suit buyers who value higher purity over width.
- Stone size should be judged with the setting and ring weight, not alone.

## 3. Purity, Weight, and Value

Ask whether the listing states karat, net gold weight, stone details, and hallmark information. Two rings with the same price can feel very different: one may spend more on a stone, while another puts more value into its gold shank.

A comfortable 14KT ring with a secure setting can be a better daily purchase than a fragile 18KT design. Confirm sizing and read the exchange policy before treating a small price difference as a bargain.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Assuming a larger-looking stone means more gold value.
- Choosing a very thin shank for rough daily work.
- Forgetting resizing, returns, or hallmark documentation.
- Comparing gold-plated and solid-gold listings as if they were equivalent.
